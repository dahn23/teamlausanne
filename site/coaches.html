Head Coach

Mariano Palena

Originally from Argentina, Mariano Palena has been coaching since the age of 18. Passionate about tennis and teaching, he supports young players throughout their athletic, mental and personal development.

“My responsibility is to help young players appreciate the process and become independent. Before being tennis players, we are people. Values always come first.”

Ranking

  • Estimated level of N4/R1 in Switzerland.

Qualifications

  • Argentine Tennis Association training, Levels 1 and 2.
  • ITF Level III.
  • Additional training in biomechanics, physical conditioning and sports psychology.

Highlights

  • Experience with juniors of all ages, including players on the ITF Junior Circuit.
  • Coached male professionals ranked as high as the ATP Top 350.
  • Supported female players who reached the WTA Top 100, attending WTA and Grand Slam tournaments.
  • International coaching experience in Argentina, Italy, Spain and Saudi Arabia.
  • Head Coach of the Sport Studies & Pro groups for more than two years.
  • Sport Studies & Pro
  • Competition
  • Performance

Loris Gander is 19 years old and has played tennis since the age of three. He trains daily in Team Lausanne Academy’s Pro pathway with the goal of becoming a professional player while sharing his passion as a coach within the academy.

Patient and passionate, Loris enjoys sharing his energy and love of tennis with the young players he coaches.

Ranking

Qualifications

Highlights

  • Occasional tournament supervision experience.

Junior Coach

Talia Picci

Talia Picci is 18 years old and is pursuing a potential career in professional tennis. Currently ranked R1, she competes in tournaments almost every weekend and is gradually beginning to compete on the international circuit.

Her coaching is based on commitment and enjoyment. When players make a strong effort, she likes to reward them with games while maintaining a relaxed and motivating atmosphere.
