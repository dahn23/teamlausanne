3. The site assistant

A bubble at the bottom right answers questions about the academy. What you should know:

  • It sees no records. No registration, no invoice, no booking, no child's file. It knows only what is published on this site, plus the dates and prices of the camps. So it can tell you nothing about your own situation — and it must not try.
  • Do not write personal data into it. No child's name, no date of birth, no telephone number, no medical question. For anything that concerns you personally, write to us at info@teamlausanne.ch.
  • Questions are kept for one year, then deleted. We use them to see what the site explains badly, and to fill the gaps.
  • Your IP address is not kept. It is turned into an unreadable fingerprint, used only to prevent abuse, and that fingerprint is erased after thirty days.
  • Questions go through Anthropic (Claude), our processor for this service, which handles them to produce the answer. They are not used to train its models.
  • It is a machine, and it can be wrong. If you are in any doubt about a price, a time or a date, the page on the site — or the office — is what counts.
Thumbs up, thumbs down. If you rate an answer, we record that vote only, attached to the question. Nothing else.

4. Minors

Registrations often concern children. They are made by a legal guardian, who is our point of contact. The assistant, for its part, must never be given information about a child.

5. Hosting and providers

  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • Supabase (European Union, eu-central-1 region) — database: registrations, requests, and the questions put to the assistant.
  • Anthropic — the language model that writes the assistant's answers.
  • Adobe Fonts and Google Fonts — the site's typefaces.

6. Cookies and trackers

No advertising cookie, no cross-site tracking. The assistant keeps your conversation's identifier in the tab's memory (sessionStorage) so the thread follows you from one page to the next; it disappears when you close the tab.

7. Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. A line to info@teamlausanne.ch is enough. For a question put to the assistant, tell us roughly when and what: we will find it and erase it.
