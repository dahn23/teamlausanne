Our product

PandaFit. Balance, measured and played.

A Bluetooth balance platform, a tablet and a panda. Employees step on for two minutes, play, and get a score. Companies get a health campaign that people actually enjoy.

PandaFit Index

A 0–100 balance score, computed from the platform's sway data. Each person keeps a personal code and follows their index over time, wherever they play.

Satisfaction Index

One quick question after each session, three buttons. A live pulse of well-being at work, without surveys nobody answers.

Games, challenges, rankings

Prevention as a game: team challenges, on-site events, champions of the day. Falls are the first cause of accidents in Switzerland; balance is trainable at any age.

Your brand, your screen

Multi-client from day one. The tablet doubles as an internal display, and the app, even the panda, can be customised to the company.
